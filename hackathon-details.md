## Requirements

- Projects must be World Cup-themed and built on X Layer.
- Projects must have a dedicated X (Twitter) account, tag `@XLayerOfficial` on submission, and post actively throughout the hackathon.
- Eligible tracks include (but are not limited to): prediction markets, trading, social, NFT, GameFi, and AI Agent.
- At least part of the project must be deployed on X Layer.
- Fully remote — no in-person attendance required.
- Existing projects are eligible, but must include substantial new development completed during the hackathon period.
- Submit the project via Google Form before 23:59 UTC on May 28.

## Rules

This hackathon uses a dual-track judging model — AI judges and human judges score independently across on-chain data, code quality, innovation, and market potential. Both scores are weighted and combined to determine final rankings.

### Scoring Weights

| Criteria               | Description                                                                                            |
| :--------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Innovation**         | Degree of differentiation within the World Cup context — mechanism, gameplay, or angle of approach.    |
| **Market potential**   | Ability to capture real World Cup traffic and convert it into X Layer on-chain users and transactions. |
| **Completion**         | Actual output delivered during the hackathon, demonstrability, and on-chain verifiability.             |
| **Demo video (bonus)** | Optional, but recommended — 1 to 3 minutes.                                                            |
